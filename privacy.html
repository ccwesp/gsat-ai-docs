GSAT-AI（學測英文）尊重並保護您的隱私。這一頁用最清楚的方式說明：您的資料去了哪裡、誰看得到。

開發者的伺服器不在這條路徑上，我們收不到、看不到、存不了。

03

AI 功能：資料怎麼走，您決定

AI MODES
Apple Intelligence 裝置端

所有處理在您的裝置上完成，不會將任何資料傳送到外部，完全離線。

雲端模型（自備 Key） 您掌控

查詢內容由您的裝置直接傳送到您選擇的供應商（Claude、OpenAI、Gemini 等）。API Key 只存在裝置的 Keychain，不會離開裝置。

自訂伺服器 您掌控

可填入自己的 OpenAI 相容伺服器（例如家中自架的 Ollama、LM Studio）。該伺服器由您自行掌控，不是開發者的伺服器。

04

相機與圖片（掃描翻譯）

CAMERA & IMAGES

拍照框選課文時，您的圖片是這樣被處理的：

  • App 只會讀取您選取的那張圖片，不會存取整個相簿。
  • 選「原生辨識」：文字辨識完全在您的裝置上進行，圖片不會離開裝置。
  • 選「AI 辨識」：您框選的圖片由裝置直接傳送到您設定的 AI 供應商或自訂伺服器。首次使用前，App 會先徵求您的同意。
  • 開發者不會接收、儲存或存取您的圖片。原始照片不會保存——只有您按「儲存」時，才會保存辨識出的文字與譯文。
05

其他說明

GSAT-AI 不包含任何第三方廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK。

GSAT-AI 不收集任何使用者資料，符合兒童隱私保護要求。實際年齡分級以 App Store 頁面標示為準。

如本政策有所更新，我們會在此頁面公布最新版本。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last Updated: November 15, 2026. GSAT-AI respects and protects your privacy.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. GSAT-AI does not collect your name, email, location, or usage behavior. No data is sent to the developer's servers.

AI Features

On-device AI (Apple Intelligence): all processing happens locally on your device.
User-provided API Keys: your queries are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you selected; the developer does not intercept or store this data. Keys are stored only in your device's Keychain.
User-configured custom server: you may use your own OpenAI-compatible server (e.g. Ollama or LM Studio on your home network). That server is controlled by you, not by the developer.

Camera and Images (Scan & Translate)

The app reads only the images you select and never accesses your whole photo library. With on-device recognition, images never leave your device. With AI recognition, the cropped area is sent directly from your device to the provider you selected (or your own custom server), after your explicit consent on first use. The developer never receives, stores, or accesses your images. Original photos are not saved; only recognized text and translations are saved, and only when you tap "Save".

Data Storage & SDKs

All learning data is stored locally on your device. Optional iCloud backup stores data in your personal iCloud account, which the developer cannot access. GSAT-AI includes no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
